About the Domus Prive

The Domus Privé is a thoughtfully designed residential and commercial development located in Seawoods, Nerul, Navi Mumbai. Designed by Atul Patel Architects (APA), the project blends modern architecture with Vastu-compliant planning. Featuring ample car parking, high-speed elevators, a recreational floor, terrace garden, and a luxurious entrance lobby, it promises refined urban living. Completion is scheduled for December 2026.

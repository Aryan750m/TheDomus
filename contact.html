Common Questions

Before you write.

We typically have a 6–10 week lead time from first contact to starting the design phase. This allows us to complete our discovery process properly. For urgent commissions, speak to us — we occasionally have capacity for a faster start.
We offer a 45-minute introductory call at no charge to understand the project and determine fit. If we proceed, the discovery phase is a paid engagement. This ensures both sides are serious before significant time is invested.
The project type (residential, hospitality), approximate size in sq.ft, location, rough budget range, and timeline are helpful. You do not need to have a clear brief — part of our value is helping define that. A few reference images or words describing how you want to feel in the space are welcome but not required.

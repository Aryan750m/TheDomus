Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. The Domus is committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency in all our interactions.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you submit an enquiry form, request a brochure, schedule a site visit, or contact us via telephone or email. This information may include your name, email address, telephone number, and property preferences.

2. Use of Information

The information collected is used solely to respond to your queries, deliver requested property brochures, provide project status updates, and coordinate site visits. We do not sell, rent, or distribute your personal data to third parties for external marketing purposes.

3. RERA and Legal Compliance

The Domus is a registered real-estate development adhering to MahaRERA guidelines (Registration No. P51700019222). All communications and records are maintained in accordance with applicable statutory regulations in Maharashtra, India.

4. Security & Data Protection

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your personal details against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

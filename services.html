What We Do

Three disciplines. One commitment.

Residence Alta — Alibaug
01

Residential Interiors

Full interior architecture for private homes — from spatial planning through material selection, bespoke joinery design, and final styling. We work on new builds and renovations of all scales, from a 1,200 sq.ft city apartment to a 10,000 sq.ft coastal estate.

Every residential commission begins with an extended site and life-study. We spend time understanding how a client actually lives before drawing a single line.

  • → Spatial planning & layout optimisation
  • → Material palette development & sourcing
  • → Bespoke furniture & joinery design
  • → Lighting design & specification
  • → Site execution & artisan management

Sable Bay Inn — Goa
02

Hospitality Design

Interiors for boutique hotels and restaurants, built around guest flow, material longevity, and sensory sequencing. We believe hospitality spaces should feel distinctive, not merely comfortable — guests should remember a specific quality of light or a particular material transition, not a generic "luxury" mood.

  • → Boutique hotels (8–40 keys)
  • → Restaurant & bar interiors
  • → Guest-flow sequencing & wayfinding
  • → Material selection for durability & atmosphere

Material Lab — Byculla Studio
03

Material Consultancy

Independent material selection and sourcing for architects and other design practices. We maintain a curated network of suppliers across India for natural stone, specialist plaster, timber, hardware, and fabric — each verified for provenance, quality, and longevity.

Our Material Lab in Byculla allows us to physically test finishes under different light conditions before specifying them. Clients and collaborators are welcome by appointment.

  • → Specification review & provenance verification
  • → Supplier network access & introductions
  • → On-site material review sessions
  • → Written material specifications & schedules

How We Work

Four stages. No shortcuts.

Stage 01

Site & Light Study

We spend a full day on site across morning and evening light before any drawing begins.

Stage 02

Spatial Concept

A single organising idea is developed and tested against every room in the brief.

Stage 03

Material Palette

Physical swatches assembled and reviewed on site, never under studio lighting alone.

Stage 04

Execution & Craft

Our site team works alongside named artisans through to final handover.

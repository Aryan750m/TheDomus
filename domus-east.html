26 EAST

Ulwe, Navi Mumbai
Ground + 12 Floors 219 Curated Units Atul Patel Architects

Two wings,
one generous vision
of connected
living.

The Domus 26 East is a contemporary twin-wing residential and commercial development located in Pushpak Node, Ulwe, Navi Mumbai. Designed by Atul Patel Architects (APA), the project balances structural symmetry with private, peaceful residential living.

26 EAST / TWIN WING DEVELOPMENT FIG. 03 — ARCHITECTURAL ELEVATION
03 The Project
Overview & Philosophy

219 addresses. One community.

Featuring a generous swimming pool, a clubhouse, dynamic podium landscape, and private decks, this twin-wing development defines modern community architectural practice.

About the Domus 26 East

The Domus 26 East is a contemporary twin-wing residential and commercial development in Pushpak Node, Navi Mumbai. Featuring spacious 2 and 3 BHK homes with integrated retail spaces, the project combines thoughtful planning with lifestyle amenities, green spaces, and quality construction. Designed by Atul Patel Architects, it is envisioned as a balanced living environment rooted in comfort, accessibility, and long-term value.

About the Domus 26 East

The Domus 26 East is a thoughtfully designed residential and commercial development located in Seawoods, Nerul, Navi Mumbai. Designed by Atul Patel Architects (APA), the project blends modern architecture with Vastu-compliant planning. Featuring ample car parking, high-speed elevators, a recreational floor, terrace garden, and a luxurious entrance lobby, it promises refined urban living. Completion is scheduled for December 2026.

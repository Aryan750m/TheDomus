How We Work

Four stages.
No shortcuts.

Each stage is grounded in site, material, and the specific life of the client.

Stage 01

Site & Light Study

We spend a full day on site across morning and evening light before any drawing begins.

Stage 02

Spatial Concept

A single organising idea is developed and tested against every room in the brief.

Stage 03

Material Palette

Physical swatches assembled and reviewed on site, never under studio lighting alone.

Stage 04

Execution & Craft

Our own site team works alongside named artisans through to final handover.

Our Material Philosophy

We design with restraint,
provenance, texture, and time.

01

Provenance

Every material is sourced with a documented origin — no unnamed veneers or unverifiable stone.

02

Restraint

A room is finished when nothing more can be removed, not when nothing more can be added.

03

Daylight-first

Layouts are modelled against real sun studies before a single finish is chosen.

04

Built to age well

We choose materials that improve with wear — waxed brass, lime plaster, oiled timber.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Honest answers to the questions clients ask most often.

A full residential interior typically spans 8–14 months from initial brief to final handover. This includes a 4–6 week discovery phase, 8–10 weeks of design development, and 6–9 months of site execution. We never rush the material-review stage.
We typically work on residential projects from 1,200 sq.ft and above, and hospitality projects of 8 rooms or more. Our Material Consultancy service has no minimum — it can be engaged for a single room or a full building.
Yes. We maintain active projects in Goa and Alibaug, and have previously delivered work in Delhi, Pune, and Bangalore. For projects outside our base cities we build in a dedicated site-travel schedule from the outset.
Our first meeting is never about mood boards. We spend 2–3 hours understanding how the client actually lives — their daily rhythms, how they move through space, what light means to them. We bring a written brief back for sign-off before any visual work begins.
We do not offer furniture-only styling. Our work begins with architecture and spatial planning; furnishings are the final layer of a considered sequence. Separating them tends to produce rooms that look curated but feel unconvincing.
